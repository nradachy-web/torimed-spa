About me

Medical aesthetician in Fredericton. Waxing is my specialty.

Hi, I’m Tori

I’ve been an aesthetician since 2021. I specialize in full body waxing, and I keep every appointment fast and efficient. I also do brow lamination, lash lifts and tints, facials, microchanneling, and gel manicures and pedicures.

I love my job because meeting you guys, making you feel your best, makes me also feel my best.

Away from the treatment room I’m at the gym, on a snowboard, playing electric guitar or reading astrology charts. I’m a Scorpio, if you were wondering.
