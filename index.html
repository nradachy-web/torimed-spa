Meet Tori

Tori Kruse has been an aesthetician since 2021. She specializes in full body waxing and keeps every appointment fast and efficient. She also does brow lamination, lash lifts and tints, facials, microchanneling, and gel manicures and pedicures.

“I love my job because meeting you guys, making you feel your best, makes me also feel my best.”

Tori Kruse

Away from the treatment room she is at the gym, on a snowboard, playing electric guitar or reading astrology charts. She is a Scorpio, if you were wondering.

Questions

Where is Tori located?

Inside The Beauty Collective at 191 Main Street, Fredericton, NB E3A 1E1. Parking is free.

I have never been waxed before. Is that okay?

Yes. First-time clients are welcome, and the review above is from a first wax. Tori keeps appointments quick, and you can message her on Instagram with any question before you book.

How should I prepare for a wax?

Tori made a short video on exactly this. Watch “How to prepare for your waxing appointment” on Instagram.

How long do brow lamination and lash lifts last?

Brow lamination lasts 4 to 6 weeks. A Korean lash lift lasts 6 to 8 weeks.

Does Tori wax men?

Yes. The menu includes back, chest and male anatomy Brazilian waxing.

Does Tori do wedding makeup?

Yes, for brides and bridal parties, and she has 2027 dates open. Choose “Wedding makeup” in the booking form.

What if I need to cancel?

Please give at least 24 hours’ notice to cancel or reschedule. Later cancellations may be charged 50% of the appointment price.
